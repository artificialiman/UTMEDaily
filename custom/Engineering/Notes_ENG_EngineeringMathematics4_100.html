Engineering · Shared Core, Year 2

Engineering Mathematics IV

Four tools carry this course: matrices for many unknowns at once, complex numbers for rotation and oscillation, residues for hard integrals, and series for equations with no closed form. Learn the tool and each problem is one or two moves. One hundred problems, every step shown.

ORIENTATION

Matrices, complex numbers, equations

A linear system, a rotation and a vibration are the same object seen three ways. A matrix holds the system, its eigenvalues give the natural modes, and a complex number carries both size and angle. Analytic functions make integrals depend only on the poles inside a loop. Differential equations, ordinary and partial, then reduce to roots, modes and recurrences.

CHAPTER 1

The Ten Blocks

One card per block, with the rule and the trap to avoid.

Block 1 · Matrices and determinants

Product, inverse, determinant

A⁻¹ = adj A ÷ det A
Rule: Multiplication is not commutative. A zero determinant means no inverse. Scaling a row scales the determinant.
Block 2 · Linear systems

Eliminate, then count rank

rank + nullity = n
Rule: A zero row with a non-zero right-hand side is no solution. A zero row with a zero right-hand side is a free parameter.
Block 3 · Eigenvalues

Solve det(A − λI) = 0

Σλ = trace, Πλ = det
Rule: Check with trace and determinant. Powers and inverses keep the eigenvectors.
Block 4 · Vector spaces

Dot, cross, project, independent

cos θ = a·b ÷ |a||b|
Rule: A cross product is perpendicular to both vectors. Gram–Schmidt subtracts projections.
Block 5 · Complex numbers

Rectangular, polar, roots

z = re^{iθ}
Rule: Pick the right quadrant for the argument. A root of order n has n values.
Block 6 · Analytic functions

Cauchy–Riemann

u_x = v_y ; u_y = −v_x
Rule: z̄ fails. A harmonic u has a conjugate v. Singularities are where the denominator vanishes.
Block 7 · Complex integration

Residues

∮ f dz = 2πi ΣRes
Rule: Only poles inside count. An analytic integrand gives 0.
Block 8 · Second-order ODE

Roots of the auxiliary equation

am² + bm + c = 0
Rule: Real, repeated or complex roots give three forms. Resonance multiplies the trial by x.
Block 9 · Partial differential equations

Classify, separate, fit the ends

u = X(x)T(t)
Rule: Boundary conditions fix the eigenvalues. Higher modes decay faster.
Block 10 · Series

Ratio test and recurrence

a_{n+1} = f(a_n)
Rule: The radius comes from the ratio test. Series solutions are built coefficient by coefficient.
