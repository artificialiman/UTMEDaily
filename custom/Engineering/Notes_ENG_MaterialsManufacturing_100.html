Engineering · Shared Core, Year 2

Engineering Materials and Manufacturing

A material is chosen by what it must survive, and a process is chosen by how the material will behave while it is shaped. Structure sets properties, properties set the process, and the process fixes the structure again. One hundred problems, every step shown.

ORIENTATION

Structure, property, process

Atoms arranged in a crystal give a strength, a stiffness and a way of failing. Heating and cooling change that structure, as the phase diagram shows. Casting, forming, machining and joining then reshape the part, and each one changes the structure it started with. Corrosion is what the environment does to the result.

CHAPTER 1

The Ten Blocks

One card per block, with the rule and the trap to avoid.

Block 1 · Stress and strain

Load per area, length per length

σ = Eε
Rule: Convert units first. Heating a fixed bar gives compression. The shear modulus and bulk modulus follow from E and ν.
Block 2 · Tensile test

Read the curve

%RA = (A₀ − A_f)/A₀
Rule: Yield and UTS use the original area. True stress is higher than engineering stress. Necking begins when true strain equals n.
Block 3 · Hardness, impact, fatigue, creep

Failure modes

σ_a = (σ_max − σ_min)/2
Rule: BCC metals turn brittle in the cold. Amplitude is half the range. Creep depends exponentially on temperature.
Block 4 · Crystal structure

Count atoms, find density

ρ = nA/(V_c N_A)
Rule: FCC has 4 atoms, BCC 2. Where atoms touch fixes a in terms of R. Miller indices are reciprocals of intercepts.
Block 5 · Defects and diffusion

Atoms move, boundaries strengthen

D = D₀ exp(−Q/RT)
Rule: Vacancies and diffusion rise sharply with temperature. A finer grain gives higher strength.
Block 6 · Phase diagrams

Use the tie line

W = (C₀ − C₁)/(C₂ − C₁)
Rule: The fraction of a phase is the far arm over the whole. At an invariant point F = 0.
Block 7 · Heat treatment

Cooling rate decides the structure

rate > (727 − T_nose)/t_nose
Rule: Quench for martensite, temper for toughness. Case depth grows as √t.
Block 8 · Material classes and composites

Mix by volume

E = V_f E_f + V_m E_m
Rule: Parallel loading is the upper bound, series the lower. A fibre carries almost all the load along itself.
Block 9 · Casting and forming

Solidify, then deform

t = B(V/A)²
Rule: Cast patterns are made larger. Rolling keeps the volume flow constant. Cold work lies below 0.4T_m.
Block 10 · Machining, joining, corrosion

Remove, join, protect

VTⁿ = C
Rule: Speed shortens tool life much more than feed. The more active metal of a galvanic couple corrodes.

Question 1
A 20 kN tensile load acts on a rod of diameter 10 mm. Find the stress.
Solution

2σ = F/A = 20 000 N ÷ 78.54 mm2 = 254.6 N/mm2.
Answer254.6 MPa
Common slips: Use the diameter squared; using the radius gives four times the stress.
